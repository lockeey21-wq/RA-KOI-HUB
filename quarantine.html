Quarantine & Acclimation

Keeping new koi separate gives you time to observe them, monitor water and reduce the risk of introducing problems to an established pond.

Simple quarantine plan

  1. Prepare a separate, cycled quarantine system before the fish arrive.
  2. Match temperature and water conditions carefully during acclimation.
  3. Observe behaviour, appetite, fins, skin and breathing.
  4. Test ammonia and nitrite regularly.
  5. Avoid routine medication without a reason or diagnosis.
  6. Only move fish to the main pond when you are confident they are healthy.
Remember: Stable water, good observation and sensible maintenance are the foundation of good koi keeping.
