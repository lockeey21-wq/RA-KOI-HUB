🩺 KOI HEALTH

Parasites & Common Problems

A reference for signs to investigate. Symptoms can overlap, so diagnosis should come before treatment whenever possible.

🔍 Flashing

Repeated rubbing or flicking against surfaces can be associated with irritation, parasites, poor water quality or other causes.

🫧 Excess mucus

Heavy slime coat, clamped fins or unusual behaviour can indicate irritation. Check water parameters first and consider experienced koi-health advice.

🦠 White spots

Small white spots can have several causes. Do not assume every white mark is the same parasite or begin medication without confirming the problem.

🐟 Ulcers & sores

Open wounds need prompt attention. Check water quality, inspect for secondary infection and seek experienced koi-health advice where needed.

Remember: Stable water, good observation and sensible maintenance are the foundation of good koi keeping.
