⚙️ POND EQUIPMENT

Filtration, Pumps & Aeration

The equipment system is the heart of a healthy koi pond. Plan the whole flow path rather than choosing components in isolation.

💨 Air

Aeration supports oxygen levels and beneficial biological activity. Position air stones where they provide useful circulation.

🔄 Pumps

Choose a pump for the required flow after allowing for pipe length, bends, filters and height. A headline pump rating is not always its real pond flow.

🧽 Mechanical filtration

Mechanical stages remove solids before they reach biological media. Easy-to-clean mechanical filtration can make routine maintenance much simpler.

🧫 Biological filtration

Biological media provides surface area for beneficial bacteria. Keep it well oxygenated and avoid damaging the biofilm unnecessarily.

🚰 Bottom drain

A well-designed bottom drain system can continuously move waste toward filtration and reduce dead spots in a pond.

🔌 Backup planning

Think about what happens during a power cut. Backup aeration can provide valuable resilience, especially during warm weather.

Remember: Stable water, good observation and sensible maintenance are the foundation of good koi keeping.
