Filtration

Good filtration is one of the foundations of healthy koi keeping.

Mechanical filtration

Removes solid waste and debris before it can break down in the system.

Biological filtration

Provides surfaces for beneficial bacteria that process toxic ammonia and nitrite.

Oxygen

Beneficial bacteria and koi both need oxygen. Strong aeration can be especially useful in heavily stocked ponds.

Maintenance

Filters need regular inspection and cleaning while avoiding unnecessary destruction of established biological media.
