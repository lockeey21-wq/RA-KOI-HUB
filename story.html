Our Story

Why R&A Koi Hub exists.

A family connection to koi

The inspiration for R&A Koi Hub goes back to your grandad Ray introducing you to koi keeping when you were around four or five. Memories of helping build his pond and spending time with the fish — especially Charlie, the friendly Chagoi — are part of what makes the hobby meaningful.

The aim is to turn that love of koi into a useful, friendly information hub for other keepers.
