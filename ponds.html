The basics

Filtration

Mechanical filtration removes solids while biological media provides surfaces for beneficial bacteria.

Bottom drains

A well-designed bottom drain can help move waste toward filtration and reduce dead spots.

Aeration

Good oxygen levels support koi and the biological filter. Extra aeration can be especially useful in warm weather.

Maintenance

Regular checks, cleaning and sensible water changes help keep a pond stable.
