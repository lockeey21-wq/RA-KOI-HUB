Building a Koi Pond

Plan the pond around the fish, filtration and long-term maintenance.

Start with the structure

A solid, level foundation is important. For a substantial koi pond, a properly designed reinforced concrete base is normally preferable to simply placing concrete directly onto loose soil.

Think about drainage

Bottom drains can make waste removal and maintenance much easier. Plan pipe routes before construction and allow suitable access to filtration equipment.

Depth and volume

Greater volume generally gives water parameters more stability. Calculate the finished internal dimensions rather than relying on rough external measurements.

Safety and maintenance

Plan safe edges, electrical supplies, pump access, overflow protection and easy routes for cleaning and water changes.
