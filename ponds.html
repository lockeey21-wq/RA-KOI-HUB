Build a pond your koi can thrive in

A good koi pond is about more than appearance. Structure, filtration, circulation, oxygen and maintenance all need to work together.

📐 Size & depth

Plan the footprint around the number and size of koi you intend to keep. Greater depth can provide more stable temperatures, but construction must suit the site.

⬇️ Bottom drains

A properly designed bottom drain can help move settled waste towards filtration and reduce dead spots.

⚙️ Filtration

Mechanical filtration removes solids; biological filtration provides surfaces for beneficial bacteria. Both matter.

💨 Aeration

Strong oxygenation is important for koi and the biological filter. Keep oxygen in mind during warm weather and overnight.

🔄 Circulation

Good circulation helps distribute oxygen and move waste. Avoid areas where debris can collect.

🧱 Construction

Foundations, walls, waterproofing and penetrations need to be designed for the loads and conditions of the site. Get structural advice where necessary.

Build safely: pond construction can involve substantial loads and water pressure. A qualified professional should assess structural work where appropriate.
