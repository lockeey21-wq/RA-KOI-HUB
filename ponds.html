Koi ponds

Planning a pond? Start with the basics: structure, depth, filtration, circulation, aeration and safe access.

Pond design

Think about location, size, depth, access to equipment and how you will maintain the pond before you build.

Filtration

Good mechanical and biological filtration helps keep water clear and provides a healthy environment for koi.

Bottom drains

Bottom drainage can help move waste towards filtration and make maintenance easier.

Aeration

Air pumps and diffusers can improve oxygen levels, especially during warm weather and overnight periods.
