Koi Ponds

Plan the pond around the fish, filtration and maintenance.

Build it properly

Plan finished water volume, depth, bottom drains, returns, oxygen, filtration access and safe electrical installation before construction begins.

  • Allow enough depth and volume for stable conditions.
  • Keep surfaces smooth and fish-safe.
  • Plan pipework before concrete.
  • Leave room around filters for maintenance.
