水質 · Suishitsu

Water quality

Healthy koi start with stable, well-maintained water. Test regularly and understand what your readings mean.

Ammonia

Ammonia is toxic to koi. Any unexpected reading should be taken seriously and investigated.

Nitrite

Nitrite is also harmful. A mature biological filter should help process nitrogenous waste.

pH

Stability is important. Test consistently and avoid making sudden changes without understanding the cause.

Water changes

Regular, sensible water changes can help manage waste and maintain water quality. Always treat replacement water appropriately.
