Water Quality

Stable water is the foundation of healthy koi.

HomeOur StoryKoiPondsWaterPlantsHealthCalculators

pH

pH measures acidity/alkalinity. Stability matters, and readings should be interpreted with the rest of the pond's chemistry.

Ammonia

Ammonia is toxic to fish. Any detectable reading deserves attention, particularly when koi are stressed.

Nitrite

Nitrite is produced during the biological conversion of ammonia and can also be harmful to koi.

Nitrate

Nitrate is a later-stage nitrogen compound. Water changes and plant uptake can help manage accumulation.

Oxygen

Warm water holds less oxygen, so aeration and good circulation become increasingly important as temperatures rise.
