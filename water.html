水質 · SUISHITSU

Water is the foundation of koi keeping

Test consistently, look for trends and avoid making sudden changes without understanding the cause.

ParameterWhy it matters
AmmoniaToxic nitrogen compound produced by fish waste and other organic material.
NitriteCan interfere with oxygen transport in fish and should be kept as low as possible.
NitrateLess immediately toxic than ammonia/nitrite but useful to monitor as a measure of nutrient accumulation.
pHAcidity/alkalinity. Stability is particularly important for koi.
KHProvides buffering capacity that helps resist sudden pH changes.
GHMeasures general hardness and reflects dissolved calcium/magnesium and other minerals.
TemperatureAffects koi metabolism, feeding, oxygen availability and biological filtration.
OxygenEssential for koi and beneficial bacteria. Warm water holds less oxygen, increasing the importance of aeration.
Good habit: record your readings with the date and time. Trends can reveal problems before they become obvious.
