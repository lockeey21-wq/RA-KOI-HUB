Water Quality

Understand the numbers that keep koi healthy.

pH

Measure acidity/alkalinity and aim for stability.

Ammonia

Toxic fish waste; should be kept at zero.

Nitrite

Toxic nitrogen compound processed by mature biofiltration.

Nitrate

End product managed with water changes and plant uptake.

KH

Helps buffer pH and supports nitrification.

Temperature

Changes metabolism, feeding and oxygen availability.

When something changes

Test first, confirm unusual results, increase oxygen if fish are distressed, and avoid making several large chemistry changes at once.
