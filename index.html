THE FISH

Koi keeping basics

Choosing koi

Learn the main varieties such as Kohaku, Sanke, Showa, Chagoi, Ogon and more. Look for healthy, active fish with good body shape, clear eyes and intact fins.

Feeding

Use quality koi food appropriate for the fish's size and season. Feed little and often, remove uneaten food and adjust quantities as temperatures change.

Growth

Growth depends on genetics, water temperature, food, oxygen, stocking density and overall husbandry. Good conditions matter more than simply feeding more.

Health

Watch for flashing, clamped fins, ulcers, unusual swimming, loss of appetite or isolation. Test the water before assuming a fish is ill.

THE POND

Build a koi pond properly

Structure

A strong base and properly supported walls help prevent movement and cracking. Plan pipework and bottom drains before concrete is poured.

Filtration

Mechanical filtration removes waste; biological media provides surfaces for beneficial bacteria. Size filtration around the real pond volume and stocking.

Bottom drain

Good circulation and a properly positioned bottom drain make waste removal much easier and help keep the pond cleaner.

Oxygen

Koi need plenty of dissolved oxygen. Air pumps and good surface movement are especially important during warm weather and at night.

WATER QUALITY

Your water is the foundation

Key tests

  • pH
  • Ammonia
  • Nitrite
  • Nitrate
  • KH / alkalinity

Golden rule

Stable, well-oxygenated water is usually more important than chasing a particular number. Test regularly and make changes gradually.

Water changes

Regular partial changes help control dissolved waste and replenish minerals. Always consider temperature and dechlorination when adding tap water.

PLANTS & GARDEN

Plants around a koi pond

Plants can make a pond look fantastic and can provide shade and habitat, but koi may uproot or eat some plants. Use protected planting areas, baskets or separate bog zones where appropriate.
