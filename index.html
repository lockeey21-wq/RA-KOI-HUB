THE STORY BEHIND R&A KOI HUB

Grandad Ray and Adam

My grandad Ray is the reason I fell in love with koi. When I was around four or five, I remember helping him build his garden koi pond and visiting my nan and grandad to see the fish.

Those early memories stayed with me. This website is a way of bringing that passion together and sharing what I learn with other koi keepers.
