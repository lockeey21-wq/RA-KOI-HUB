Our Story

My grandad Ray is the reason I got into koi. From a young age, I remember helping him build his garden pond and visiting Nan and Grandad to see the fish. He had a friendly Chagoi called Charlie, who loved being hand-fed and having his head rubbed.

This website is a little tribute to those memories, while sharing what I learn about koi keeping with other people.
