OUR STORY

Grandad Ray & Adam's Koi Hub

My grandad Ray is the reason I got into koi. From being around four or five years old, I remember helping him build his garden koi pond and visiting Nan and Grandad to see the fish.

Those memories stayed with me and grew into a real passion for koi. This hub is a way of bringing that passion together and sharing useful information with other koi keepers.
