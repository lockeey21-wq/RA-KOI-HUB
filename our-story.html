Our Story

My grandad Ray is the reason I got into koi. From being around four or five years old, I remember helping him build his garden koi pond and visiting Nan and Grandad to see the fish.

Those memories stayed with me and grew into a real passion for koi keeping. This website is a way of bringing that passion together and sharing useful information with other koi keepers.

Grandad Ray & Adam's Koi Hub is dedicated to the hobby, the fish and the memories that started it all.
