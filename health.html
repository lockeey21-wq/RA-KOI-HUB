Know your koi

Healthy koi should be observed regularly. Behaviour, appetite, swimming, skin and water quality all provide clues.

👀 Watch behaviour

Look for flashing, rubbing, isolation, clamped fins, unusual swimming, surface gasping or rapid breathing.

🔎 Physical changes

Check for ulcers, sores, redness, raised scales, excess mucus, damaged fins or other changes from normal appearance.

🧪 Test the water

Many koi health problems are linked to water quality. Test before assuming a disease or parasite is the cause.

🦠 Parasites

Different parasites have different signs and treatments. Where possible, diagnosis should come before medication.

🏠 Quarantine

A separate quarantine setup can allow observation of new or unwell koi and reduce risk to the main pond.

👨‍⚕️ Get expert help

For serious illness, repeated deaths or uncertain diagnosis, speak with an experienced koi health professional or aquatic veterinarian.

Important: medication should be used according to the product instructions and appropriate professional advice. Avoid mixing treatments unless you know they are compatible.
