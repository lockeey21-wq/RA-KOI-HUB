Koi Health

Observe first, test the water, then investigate.

Behaviour

Watch feeding response, swimming, flashing, isolation and unusual surface behaviour.

Appearance

Look for sores, ulcers, clamped fins, abnormal patches, damaged scales or rapid changes.

First checks

Check temperature and water parameters before assuming a disease or parasite is responsible.

Quarantine

New or unwell fish may need isolation. Seek qualified aquatic-veterinary advice for persistent or serious problems.
