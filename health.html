Koi health

Spotting changes early can make a big difference. Observe behaviour, appearance, appetite and water quality together.

Warning signs

Look for flashing, clamped fins, unusual isolation, poor appetite, ulcers, abnormal swimming or rapid breathing.

Quarantine

A quarantine setup can help you observe new or unwell koi separately and reduce the risk of introducing problems to the main pond.

Parasites

Different parasites require different identification and treatment. Avoid treating blindly where possible.

When to get help

For serious illness, unexplained deaths or uncertain diagnosis, seek advice from an experienced koi health professional or aquatic veterinarian.
