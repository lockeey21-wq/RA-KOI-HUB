Koi Health

Recognising problems and starting with the basics.

Educational guide: serious illness needs experienced koi health or veterinary advice.

First checks

  1. Test water quality and temperature.
  2. Observe breathing, swimming and feeding.
  3. Consider recent changes or new fish.
  4. Increase oxygen if fish are gasping while investigating the cause.
  5. Avoid stacking treatments without a reason or diagnosis.
