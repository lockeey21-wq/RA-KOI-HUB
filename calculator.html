Pond Volume Calculator

For a rectangular pond, multiply length × width × average depth in metres, then multiply by 1,000 for approximate litres.

Example: 2m × 1.5m × 0.8m = 2,400 litres.

Actual volume will be lower after accounting for shelves, rocks, filters and other equipment.
