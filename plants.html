水草 · Mizukusa

Pond plants

Plants can add natural beauty and shade, but koi may eat or disturb many pond plants, so plant choice and protection matter.

Water lily

Beautiful floating leaves and flowers. Protect roots and crowns from curious koi where needed.

Water iris

A popular marginal plant for adding height around a pond edge.

Hornwort

An oxygenating plant that can grow submerged. Koi may pull at loose pieces.

Floating plants

Floating cover can provide shade, but monitor growth so the pond surface is not excessively covered.
