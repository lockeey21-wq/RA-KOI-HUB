Plants, shade and natural beauty

Plants can provide shade and visual interest, but koi can uproot or eat many varieties.

🌸 Water lily

Floating leaves can provide shade and flowers add colour. Protect the root zone from koi where necessary.

🌿 Water iris

A popular marginal plant that can add height and structure around the pond edge.

🌱 Hornwort

A submerged oxygenating plant. Loose stems can be moved around by active koi.

🍃 Floating plants

Can provide cover and shade. Monitor growth so the surface does not become excessively covered.

🪴 Plant baskets

Heavy planting baskets and suitable protection can make it harder for koi to disturb roots and soil.

☀️ Shade

Planting and other shade solutions can help reduce intense sunlight and give koi sheltered areas.
