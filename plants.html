Pond Plants

Plants can add colour, shade and natural character to a pond.

Choosing plants for koi

Koi can uproot or eat many plants, so protect roots and choose planting areas carefully. Floating plants can provide shade but should never cover the entire surface.

Examples

Water lilies, marginal plants and oxygenating plants can all have a place in a pond, depending on depth, sunlight and maintenance needs.

Keep balance

Remove dying leaves and control excessive growth so the pond remains healthy and equipment remains accessible.
