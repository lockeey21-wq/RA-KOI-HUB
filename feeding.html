Feeding Koi

Good feeding is about growth, colour, digestion and water quality — not simply giving as much food as possible.

🍚 Choose the right food

Use a quality koi food suited to the season and the size of your fish. Small fry need appropriately sized particles so they can eat comfortably.

⏱️ Feed little and often

Several small feeds can work well for growing koi. Only offer what the fish can consume promptly, then adjust the amount as temperature and appetite change.

🌡️ Watch the temperature

Koi digestion and appetite are strongly affected by water temperature. As water cools, feeding should be reduced and eventually stopped when appropriate.

💧 Protect water quality

Uneaten food breaks down and adds to the biological load. Good filtration, aeration and regular testing should go hand in hand with feeding.

Remember: Stable water, good observation and sensible maintenance are the foundation of good koi keeping.
