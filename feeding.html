Feeding Koi

Feed for health, growth and good water quality.

Simple rules

  • Use food suited to fish size and season.
  • Feed small amounts and watch appetite.
  • Remove or avoid excess uneaten food.
  • Adjust feeding as water temperature changes.
  • Remember that more food means more waste for filtration.
