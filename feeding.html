Feeding Koi

Feed for growth, condition and water quality — not simply because the fish ask for food!

Small and growing koi

Use an appropriately sized quality koi food. Smaller fish need smaller particles so they can eat comfortably.

How much?

Feed only what the fish can consume promptly. Adjust portions for temperature, appetite, growth and water quality.

Cold weather

As water temperature falls, koi metabolism and digestion slow down. Feeding should be reduced and eventually stopped when conditions are unsuitable.
