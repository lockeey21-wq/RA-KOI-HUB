Winter Care

Koi can overwinter outdoors in the UK when the pond is properly planned and maintained.

Temperature matters

As water cools, koi become less active and require less food. Avoid feeding simply because the fish appear interested.

Keep oxygen available

Maintain sensible aeration and ensure there is adequate gas exchange at the surface. Avoid completely sealing the pond.

Monitor the pond

Continue checking water quality and equipment through winter. Protect pumps, pipework and exposed equipment from freezing where necessary.
