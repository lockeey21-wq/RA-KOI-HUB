Grandad Ray & Adam's Koi Hub

Where the koi journey began

My grandad Ray is the reason I fell in love with koi. From helping him build his garden pond when I was around four or five, to visiting my grandparents and seeing the fish, those memories stayed with me.

R&A Koi Hub is built to share that passion with other koi keepers and to keep that family connection at the heart of the project.

A family passion

This website is a growing resource for koi keepers — covering koi varieties, pond building, water quality, plants, health and everyday care.

Grandad Ray and Adam's Koi Hub is about learning, sharing and enjoying koi keeping.
