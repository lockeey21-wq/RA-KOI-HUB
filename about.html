Our Story

R&A Koi Hub is a place for koi keepers, beginners and anyone who loves these amazing fish.

Where it started

My love of koi started when I was around 4 or 5 years old. My grandad Ray introduced me to koi keeping. I remember helping him build his koi pond and visiting my grandparents to see the fish.

One koi in particular always stood out — a big, friendly Chagoi called Charlie. Charlie loved being hand-fed and even enjoyed head rubs.

That early experience is a big part of why R&A Koi Hub exists today: to share the enjoyment of koi keeping and help other people learn.

What you'll find here

Guides covering koi varieties, pond construction, filtration, water quality, plants, feeding, winter care and practical koi keeping.
