GRANDAD RAY & ADAM

The story behind the Hub

R&A Koi Hub started with family, memories and a love of koi.

Grandad Ray

My grandad Ray is the reason I got into koi. When I was around four or five, I remember helping him build his garden koi pond and going to my nan and grandad's house to see the fish.

I remember the enjoyment of being around the pond and learning from those early experiences. Koi became something that stayed with me.

R&A Koi Hub is my way of building something around that passion and keeping the memory of Grandad Ray at the heart of it.

Why the Hub exists

The aim is to create a useful, easy-to-understand resource for koi keepers of all experience levels — from someone setting up their first pond to keepers who have been doing it for years.
