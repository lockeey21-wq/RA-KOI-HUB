Koi Varieties

A simple guide to some of the best-known varieties.

Kohaku

White koi with red patterning. One of the classic varieties.

Sanke

A white-based koi with red and black patterning.

Showa

A black-based koi carrying red and white patterning.

Chagoi

Brown, bronze or olive-toned koi, often known for becoming very friendly.

Ochiba

A grey or blue base with brown/orange patterning.

Shiro Utsuri

Black and white koi with bold contrasting pattern.
