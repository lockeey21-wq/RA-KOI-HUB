Koi Varieties

A quick guide to some of the most popular varieties.

Kohaku

White koi with red patterning. One of the classic varieties.

Sanke

White, red and black patterned koi.

Showa

Black-based koi with red and white patterning.

Chagoi

Brown, bronze or olive-toned koi, often known for becoming very friendly and confident.

Ogon

Metallic single-colour koi, commonly golden or platinum.

Asagi

Blue-scaled koi with red colouring developing around the lower body.
