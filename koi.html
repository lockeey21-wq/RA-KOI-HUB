Koi Varieties & Care

Kohaku

White koi with red patterning. Look at pattern balance, skin quality and body shape.

Showa

Black-based koi with red and white markings. Pattern and development can change as the fish grows.

Chagoi

Brown, bronze or olive-toned koi often known for becoming confident around people.
