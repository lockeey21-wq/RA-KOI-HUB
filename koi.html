Koi varieties

Explore the most popular koi varieties and learn what makes each one special.

Kohaku

White koi with red markings. One of the classic Gosanke varieties.

Sanke

A white koi with red and black patterning.

Showa

A black-based koi featuring red and white pattern.

Chagoi

Brown, earthy-toned koi known for their friendly nature and strong growth.

Asagi

A blue-scaled koi with distinctive red coloration along the sides.

Ogon

Metallic single-colour koi, commonly seen in bright gold or platinum.
