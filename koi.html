錦鯉 · KOI VARIETIES

Get to know your koi

Koi come in a huge range of colours, patterns and scale types. Here are some of the varieties every keeper should know.

Kohaku

White koi with red patterning. Pattern quality, skin and balance are major parts of what makes a Kohaku special.

Taisho Sanke

White-based koi with red and black markings. Black should generally sit over the white rather than dominating the head.

Showa

A black-based koi with red and white pattern. Strong Showa can have dramatic sumi wrapping around the body.

Chagoi

Earthy brown/bronze koi. Often known for confidence and becoming very tame around their keeper.

Asagi

Blue reticulated scales with red coloration along the sides. A classic and distinctive koi.

Ogon

Metallic single-colour koi, commonly gold or platinum, valued for clean colour and shine.

Shiro Utsuri

Black and white koi with a striking pattern. The contrast between sumi and white creates the visual impact.

Kujaku

Metallic koi with a net-like pattern over the scales and red/orange patterning.

Bekko

Single-colour based koi with contrasting black markings, commonly seen in white, red or yellow forms.
